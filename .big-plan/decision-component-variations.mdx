## What you are comparing

_Read each one cold and notice how much work it takes to answer._

Every matrix below renders the same decision with the same words.
Only the arrangement differs, so any difference in header legibility is the arrangement's doing.

Round 5 also applies three shared refinements to the real `Decision` component:

- Every row-layout option name has a rule beneath it, with smaller criteria copy below.
- Proposing another approach reveals an explicit **Cancel** action that restores the prior selection.
- The brief's **Compare all three** label is vertically centered in its collapsed row.

## The component model

_Five instances of one component, not five components._

Every surface in this review exercises the built-in `Decision` component through temporary comparison layouts:

- Shared row hierarchy is `<Decision layout="rows">`.
- Matrix 1 is `<Decision layout="matrix-wide">`.
- Matrix 2 is `<Decision layout="matrix-transposed">`.
- Matrix 3 is `<Decision layout="matrix-keyed">`.
- The disclosure check is `<Decision layout="brief">`.

They are not `SmallDecision`, `ComplexDecision`, or separate component types.

The already-approved component boundary remains: `Decision` asks the reviewer a question and ends in a confirmed answer; `ComplexDecision` records a weighted analysis for the reviewer to audit. They stay two components because they own different jobs.

Their matrix-shaped presentations share the internal `ComparisonMatrix` primitive so the two components do not drift into different visual dialects. All three matrix directions below and the brief's on-demand comparison use that primitive; the row layout is the non-matrix presentation of `Decision`.

## How to choose

_Pick the matrix whose trade is cheapest for real plan content._

1. **Wide breakout** preserves the familiar matrix but spends document width.
2. **Options as rows** gives names the most natural reading position but turns criteria sideways.
3. **Keyed chooser rail** keeps the grid compact but asks the reader to carry A/B/C.
4. Every direction keeps native radio behavior, stable rationale height, proposal cancellation, and the shared matrix primitive.
