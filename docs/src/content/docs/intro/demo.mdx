The viewer follows your OS preference and remembers your choice when you override it.
[See the full rendered plan in your browser.](/demo/)

Some parts of this plan are plain-old markdown.

But take a look at the **code diff** and **HTTP endpoint** sections.
Those are examples of [big plan components](/components/) that are specially designed to render common types of information in a first-class way.
Big Plan ships today with over a dozen components, and because it's open source, it's easy to add more as we find the need.

But wait, there's more!
You can also comment on specific parts of a big-plan, track plan versions, navigate the plan with vim shortcuts, and enforce plan templates.
Plus, it all runs locally without the need for any third-party service.
To see it in action, [install it](/intro/installation/).
