title: Your first review
description: Ask your agent for a plan, read it in your browser, talk it through, and approve it.
---

Your first review takes about five minutes. You will not type a single command — you ask your
agent, and it does the rest.

## 1. Ask your agent for a plan

Before it starts building something, say:

```text
Write a plan for this with Big Plan, then open it for review.
```

Your agent writes the plan and starts Big Plan, which opens the plan in your browser as a
document made for reading.

## 2. Read it

You get a real document: a table of contents, sections you can collapse, and the diagrams,
diffs, tables, and schemas the plan needs — not a wall of Markdown in a terminal.

Read it the way you would read anything. The point of the next few minutes is to find out whether
you actually agree with the approach.

## 3. Talk it through

Select any sentence and choose **Comment**. Ask whatever you want — "why this order?", "what
happens if this fails?" Your agent answers in the thread, right next to the thing you asked
about.

The plan has questions for you too. When you reach a decision card, pick the option you want.

Two ways to change the plan, and you can mix them freely:

- **Ask your agent** — "swap the queue for a cron job" — and it revises the plan, which updates
  in front of you.
- **Use the review yourself** — answer decisions and leave comments directly in the document.

## 4. Approve it

When you are happy with the approach, choose **Approve plan**.

Your answers are written into the plan file, the agent gets your approval note, and it can start
building — against a plan you have actually read and agreed with.

That is the whole loop.
