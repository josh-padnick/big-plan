Review a retry-queue rollout

Use the thin thread kernel to leave durable notes before the retry queue ships.

Quick summary

Why
Failed payments need one visible recovery path.
What
Add a bounded queue with explicit ownership and operator recovery.
How
Persist attempts, lease one job at a time, and stop after the declared budget.
Part 1Proposal

1.1 / One queue, one owner

One queue, one owner

The worker owns retry timing; checkout only records the failed attempt.

The API writes a job and returns immediately. A worker leases the oldest ready job, applies exponential backoff, and records the terminal outcome.

Field Meaning
attempt Number of completed delivery attempts
readyAt Earliest time another worker may lease the job
leaseOwner Worker currently responsible for the attempt

Part 2Shipping

2.1 / Acceptance criteria

Ready when the loop is honest

  • Every failed payment has one durable state.
  • A lease expiry makes the job available without duplicating a successful send.
  • Exhausted jobs remain visible to an operator.
  • Metrics distinguish queued, leased, retried, succeeded, and exhausted work.
