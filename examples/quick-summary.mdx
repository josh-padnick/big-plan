# Retry Pipeline Summary Plan

A persistent retry queue replaces inline payment-capture retries, summarized here with the `QuickSummary` component.

<QuickSummary>

<What>

- Failed captures move from inline API-server retries into a persistent queue with explicit state.

</What>

<How>

- A worker drains the queue with bounded, jittered backoff.
- Every attempt lands in an operator-visible audit trail.

</How>

<Risks>

- Double charges if capture and schedule updates are not transactional.

</Risks>

<Decisions>

- The queue technology, and whether rollout starts with a shadow mode.

</Decisions>

</QuickSummary>

## Why the queue

Inline retries couple checkout latency to processor health and lose retry state on every deploy.
A persistent queue makes retry state durable, observable, and independent of request lifecycles.

## Acceptance criteria

- A failed capture is retried on schedule after an API-server restart.
- Operators can pause, force, and cancel retries per merchant.
