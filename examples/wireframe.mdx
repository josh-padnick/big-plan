# Eddy's Wallet Child Experience

A kid-safe wallet gets its own screens, sketched here with `Wireframe` so the shape of the experience is arguable before any of it is built.

## The wallet and its two child-sized tasks

The wallet leads with what Eddy has and what he is saving for. Asking a grown-up and learning about a loan each become a short sequence with one job, one obvious next action, and a deliberate ending.

## Why this prototype is longer

The two risky moments are now explicit task flows instead of overloaded destination screens. The child chooses, supplies only the information needed, sees the exact outcome, and crosses a clear handoff or completion boundary before returning to the wallet.
