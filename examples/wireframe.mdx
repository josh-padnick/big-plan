# Eddy's Wallet Child Experience

A kid-safe wallet gets its own screens, sketched here with `Wireframe` so the shape of the experience is arguable before any of it is built.

## The wallet home

A child opens the app to one question: how much do I have, and how close am I to the thing I am saving for.

Everything else on the screen is secondary, so balance and goal own the widest region and the lesson prompt sits beside them.

## Why four screens

The lesson and grown-up handoff are the two places the child leaves the wallet, so the prototype walks both paths and returns.

A reviewer can click through it here rather than imagining it, which is the whole point of drawing it at all.
