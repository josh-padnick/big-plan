# Harbor across three form factors

Sketch the Harbor support inbox natively for desktop, tablet, and phone.

Each form factor is a parallel prototype so reviewers compare real device layouts rather than one layout with different captions.

## Desktop web SaaS

One consistent pane model: fixed global nav, then workspace panes (list, main, details).
Content hierarchy comes from type and layout; major panes use dividers rather than competing card outlines.

### Ticket - design rationale (priority 1)

- **Workspace:** one bounded app viewport; queue, conversation, and properties scroll independently, with the conversation dominant and its composer sticky.
- **Modes:** Reply names the customer-visible audience; Internal note names the team-only audience. The mode owns placeholder, reassurance, and send label.
- **State:** Properties is the single status owner; Resolve is a clearly subordinate action there, not a competing header state.
- **Keyboard:** list movement, open, search, composer focus, mode switches, and send remain visible and learnable.

### Inbox - design rationale

- **Comparison:** every row keeps ticket, status, assignee, and updated time aligned; selection uses tint plus an inset edge.
- **Judgment:** the preview earns its rail with the latest message, SLA urgency, owner, customer context, recommendation, and a route into the conversation.
- **Views vs filters:** saved views (All / Mine / Unassigned) stay separate from removable filter chips.
- **Toolbar:** one sticky search/filter/sort row and one result count; SLA copy says **due in 4m** or **breached**, never elapsed/target arithmetic.

### New ticket - design rationale

- **Facts:** contact and organization are explicit, followed by required subject and description.
- **Recommendations:** duplicate evidence explains why it matched; actions say **Review ticket**, **Create and link**, or **Not a duplicate** while preserving the draft.
- **Routing:** Team, assignee, and priority stay together; Suggested includes provenance and becomes an ordinary value when accepted.
- **Side effects:** the confirmation and final button both say the ticket is created and Maya is emailed.

### Settings - design rationale

- **Scope:** workspace defaults say they affect everyone; personal delivery says it affects only Alex.
- **Dependencies:** Slack channel and digest time sit indented beneath their parent switches; disabled state remains visible.
- **Outcomes:** labels say who receives what and when, while switches reinforce state with On/Off text.
- **Save model:** one sticky unsaved-changes bar owns Discard and Save.

## Phone

Triage, reply, and capture - not a compressed desktop.
Whole-row opens, vertical message timeline, light tab bar with active states, and recovery without permanent explanatory chrome.

### Ticket - design rationale (priority 1)

- **Timeline:** stacked `Message` blocks (customer / agent / internal), body under sender+time.
- **Composer:** Reply | Internal note segment; **Send reply** / sticky above safe area; attachment affordance; internal mode stays tinted.
- **Chrome:** `‹ Inbox` back, overflow for resolve/status; **Inbox tab stays active** (came from inbox).
- **No permanent** undo/draft process copy - snackbar only after send (not drawn as standing helper text).

### Inbox / New / Settings

- Inbox titled **Inbox** with open count; saved views and the active filter share one control group; two-line rows; row is the action.
- New ticket: sticky **Create ticket**, Cancel, draft on New tab, and separate form, attachment, possible-match, and additional-detail groups.
- Settings: grouped overview with current values; blocked notifications open an actionable detail; account and workspace actions are distinct.
