# Using QuickSummary well

The card a reviewer reads first, and the only part many will remember.
Place it directly after the lede, before any section.

- The enforced shape: `What`, then optionally `How` and `OpenQuestions` sections in that order, each holding one bullet list of at most 3 bullets, at most 600 readable characters in total, one QuickSummary per plan.
- `What` states the change a reviewer could repeat to a colleague; `How` gives the shape of the solution; `OpenQuestions` lists what the reviewer must answer, each bullet phrased as a question ending with a question mark.
- Fold risks into the open questions: the question that answers a risk carries the risk with it.
- Summarize at the altitude of intent, not inventory: if a bullet names a file path, generator, or command flag, it is detail that belongs in a section below.
- One line per bullet, and nothing essential may appear for the first time after the summary.
