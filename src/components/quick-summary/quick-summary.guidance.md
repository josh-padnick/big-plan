# Using QuickSummary well

The card a reviewer reads first, and the only part many will remember.
Place it directly after the lede, before any section.

- The enforced shape: exactly one bullet list, at most 5 bullets, at most 600 characters of readable text, one QuickSummary per plan.
- Summarize at the altitude of intent, not inventory: if a bullet names a file path, generator, or command flag, it is detail that belongs in a section below.
- Cover, roughly in order: what changes, the shape of the solution, the decisions you need from the reviewer, and the biggest risk.
- One line per bullet; cut connective words before cutting substance.
- Nothing essential may appear for the first time after the summary, and nothing in the summary may be a mystery until a later section explains it.
