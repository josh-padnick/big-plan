# Using Part well

The chapter break of the deck: a self-closing divider that groups the slides after it into one act.
Write `<Part title="Context" />` between sections; it takes no body content.

- Use around three acts per plan: context, the proposal, and shipping plus the reviewer's calls; adapt the names when the plan demands it.
- Part 1 canonically opens with "Status quo" and "Success looks like", so the reviewer knows today's reality and the outcome before any design; typed "Desired experience" or "Desired outcome" slides are light alternatives when their framing fits.
- Big Plan numbers parts in document order and derives every slide's kicker from them, so never write numbers into the titles.
- Keep titles short act names, not sentences; "The proposal" reads better than "What I am going to build and why".
- A short plan with only a handful of sections may not need parts at all; do not add dividers a reader would step over.
