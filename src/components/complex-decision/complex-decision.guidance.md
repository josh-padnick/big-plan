# Using ComplexDecision well

One weighty tradeoff the reviewer must settle, with options scored against criteria and a recommendation.

- Reach for it only when options genuinely need side-by-side scoring across shared criteria; the matrix is the heaviest read in Big Plan. Prefer `Decision` for most tradeoffs.
- Give every option an honest summary and score; a card with one real option and two strawmen wastes the reviewer's trust.
- Mark your recommendation, and keep the deciding context in the card rather than scattered through nearby sections.
- Two or three per plan is a lot; if everything is a ComplexDecision, nothing is.
